Story one. Joy Online and Three News report that businessman Alfred Agbesi Woyome has submitted his nomination forms to run for the position of National Vice Chairperson of the National Democratic Congress. Story two. Pressure is growing on authorities regarding sexual assault allegations against the Headmaster of Obuasi Senior High Technical School, according to Joy Online and The Ghana Report. Joy Online reports that National Democratic Congress Chairman Johnson Asiedu Nketiah has been elected the inaugural Chair of the African Political Parties Initiative at a summit in Addis Ababa, Ethiopia. In his acceptance speech, he urged political organizations across the continent to build strong institutions and look beyond simply winning elections.

Story four. Three News reports that Charles Eric Kofi Wayo, the outspoken politician, businessman, and founder of the United Renaissance Party, has died at the age of seventy-one at a clinic in Accra.
