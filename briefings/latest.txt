Good evening. On this Thursday evening, here is your political news briefing. First, the news from home.

Story one: The N P P has announced that prospective parliamentary aspirants for the 2028 general elections must formally declare their intention between November 1st and November 10th. Graphic Online and MyJoyOnline report that the party's General Secretary, Justin Kodua Frimpong, stated that all prospective candidates, including sitting Members of Parliament, will also pay a five thousand cedi fee for early eligibility checks.

Story two: The N P P has filed a case at the Supreme Court seeking a formal interpretation of the constitutional limits on presidential terms. According to The Ghana Report and Graphic Online, the party is asking the court to rule that the two-term limit applies to both consecutive and non-consecutive terms, meaning a person cannot be elected President of Ghana more than twice.

Story three: A debate has emerged over the leadership of the minority caucus in Parliament. MyJoyOnline reports that while the N P P has denied reports of plans to replace Minority Leader Alexander Afenyo-Markin, the N D C's Rashid Tanko Computer dismissed the denial as mere firefighting and claimed the matter has been under discussion since the 2024 elections.
