Good afternoon. It is Sunday, October eleventh. 

First, the news from home.

Story one. The New Patriotic Party has ordered all prospective parliamentary aspirants, their agents, and supporters to immediately suspend all campaign activities. According to MyJoyOnline, the party warned that anyone who ignores the directive faces disciplinary action that could disqualify them from contesting.

Story two. President Mahama has revealed that the United States wants to hold talks to revise its proposed one hundred and nine million dollar health compact with Ghana. Three News reports that the discussions will focus on amending or removing certain clauses that had previously been rejected by the Ghanaian Cabinet.

Story three. Ghana has been upgraded from Tier Two to Tier One in the United States Department of State Trafficking in Persons Report. Three News reports that the International Justice Mission Ghana has congratulated the government and key stakeholders for this historic achievement in combating human trafficking.

Story four. Graphic Online reports that Northern Regional Minister Ali Adolf John has praised Minister of Finance Cassiel Ato Forson for improving the release of the District Assemblies Common Fund. Speaking to local leaders in Tamale, he said the timely disbursement of the funds has brought significant relief to assemblies across the region.

Now, around the world.

Story five. Tensions have risen between Washington and Kyiv after United States President Donald Trump said it is time for Ukraine to get a new president who will agree to end the war. According to reports from the B B C and France Twenty-Four, the remarks followed Ukraine's criticism of an American deal to release Russian diesel, while President Volodymyr Zelensky stated he is willing to stop striking Russian refineries only if Moscow ceases its attacks on Ukrainian energy infrastructure.

Story six. Poland's police and security officials are investigating an alleged plot to assassinate Prime Minister Donald Tusk and his defence minister. Al Jazeera and The Guardian report that the government held an emergency meeting over the threat amid heightened concerns of hybrid attacks aimed at undermining European support for Ukraine.
