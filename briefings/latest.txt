First, the news from home.

Story one. MyJoyOnline reports that the Minority Caucus on Parliament's Foreign Affairs Committee is demanding an immediate suspension of Ghana's maritime cooperation and visa-waiver arrangement with Colombia. Story two. Starr FM and Three News report that the Office of the Attorney General and the Economic and Organised Crime Office have defended the arrest of Manhyia South Member of Parliament, Nana Agyei Baffour Awuah. Story three. Three News reports that President John Mahama has convened a crucial meeting with striking teacher unions, the Education Ministry, and the Ghana Education Service. The meeting aims to discuss their demands and evaluate the implications for the national compensation budget.

Story four. Three News reports that Ghana has won a major international arbitration case in London. The tribunal dismissed Tullow Oil's claims and fully upheld the Ghana Revenue Authority's three hundred and ninety-three million dollar tax assessment.
