Good evening, and welcome to your news update for Saturday night.

First, the news from home.

Story one. The National Democratic Congress has ordered all aspirants and members taking part in its ongoing reorganisation to remove oversized posters and banners within five days. According to MyJoyOnline, Starr F M, and The Ghana Report, the party's Functional Executive Committee has restricted campaign materials to a maximum size of five feet wide by eight feet high and warned that anyone who fails to comply will face disciplinary action.

Story two. Fresh debate has broken out over Ghana's foreign policy following revelations about past and present moves to join the B R I C S economic bloc. Reporting by Three News highlights comments by Member of Parliament Samuel Okudzeto Ablakwa detailing that former President Akufo-Addo's administration studied the benefits of joining, while critics such as Fiifi Boafo argue that considering membership in the past does not justify President Mahama's current decisions.

Story three. Following the recent New Patriotic Party national delegates conference, newly elected National Chairman John Boadu has pledged to foster internal unity and build strong structures ahead of the twenty twenty-eight elections. Three News and The Ghana Report note that he led newly elected executives, including First National Vice Chairman Kwadwo Bamba, to visit former President John Agyekum Kufuor.

Story four. Pressure continues to mount on the government over a controversial visa issue as parliamentary voices and civil society figures demand stronger action. According to Three News, legal practitioner Kofi Bentil and the Minority in Parliament are calling for more dismissals, including demands for the removal of the Minister for Sports and Recreation, Kofi Adams, and the head of the Ghana Tourism Authority.

Now, around the world.

Story five. In Sudan, human rights monitors report that a drone strike launched by paramilitary forces has killed forty-one people near the border with Ethiopia, including eight children. According to B B C News Africa, the strike targeted a gathering of displaced civilians as the country's brutal conflict continues to take a heavy toll on non-combatants.

Story six. International diplomatic tensions have escalated dramatically after United States President Donald Trump announced an agreement to import diesel from Russia. Reports from the B B C, Al Jazeera, and The Guardian state that Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy sharply condemned the deal as funding Moscow's war effort, prompting Trump to declare that Ukraine needs a new president who will agree to end the conflict.
