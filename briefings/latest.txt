Good afternoon. It is Friday, and here is your political briefing.

First, the news from home.

Story one. In internal party developments within the National Democratic Congress, David Selassie Anthony, known as Dee Mayor, has officially declared his bid to run for Deputy National Organiser, promising to empower grassroots members and regional organisers. Both Graphic Online and MyJoyOnline covered the announcement, which comes as thirty party members in the Upper West Region and forty-nine in the Eastern Region have filed nomination papers for regional executive races. 

Story two. Graphic Online reports that the Majority Caucus on Parliament’s Foreign Affairs Committee has defended spending forty-nine point seven two million Ghana Cedis to evacuate nearly two thousand Ghanaians from South Africa. The caucus described the ministry's financial account as unprecedented and transparent, and backed an independent audit of evacuation exercises by the Auditor-General dating back to 2017.

Story three. Starr F M reports that the Coalition of Domestic Election Observers has demanded the resignation of Emelia Agbenyo as Deputy Chairperson of the Electoral Commission. The election observer group warned that her reported campaign activities with the N D C raise serious concerns about the commission's impartiality and could undermine public trust in future elections.

Story four. Graphic Online reports that veteran journalist Kwesi Pratt Junior has advised the N D C leadership to document its history accurately and acknowledge the sacrifices of its ordinary supporters rather than focusing only on prominent figures. Meanwhile, MyJoyOnline reports that party operative James Agbey has similarly warned that the party risks losing its base if government appointees fail to address complaints of neglect among ordinary workers.

Now, around the world.

Story five. France twenty-four and the B B C report that Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky is meeting European Union leaders in Brussels to rally support amid deepening tensions with Washington. United States President Donald Trump openly criticised Zelensky, calling him harder to deal with than Russian President Vladimir Putin, following disputes over Ukrainian strikes on Russian oil refineries and US sanctions policy.

Story six. Africanews reports that United States Secretary of State Marco Rubio has spoken directly with Ethiopian Prime Minister Abiy Ahmed, urging him to de-escalate tensions and pursue dialogue with neighbouring Eritrea. Washington is pressing both governments to maintain their commitments under the historic 2018 peace deal as diplomatic friction grows across the Horn of Africa.
