Story one. Campaign activities within the National Democratic Congress are picking up as candidates reach out to delegates. Story two. In Parliament, the Member of Parliament for Mpraeso, Davis Ansah Opoku, has criticized executive security agencies over illicit drug trafficking. Story three. On local governance policy, Deputy Minister for Local Government Rita Naa Odoley Sowah has directed district chief executives to ensure strict accountability in public spending. Graphic Online reports that she urged local government heads to properly utilize their eighty percent allocation from the District Assemblies Common Fund for key infrastructure projects.
