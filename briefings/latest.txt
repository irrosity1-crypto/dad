Good afternoon. Today is Saturday, November 28. First, the news from home.

Story one: Starr F M and 3News report that Minority Leader Afenyo-Markin raised concerns in Parliament over the return of power outages, known as dumsor. In response, the Energy Ministry admitted local grid challenges exist and ordered a full audit, while a World Bank review rated Ghana's energy recovery plan as unsatisfactory due to administrative delays.

Story two: Starr F M reports that the Commission on Human Rights and Administrative Justice has cleared former President John Mahama, Naana Jane Opoku-Agyemang, and Ibrahim Mahama of all corruption allegations. The commission stated it found no evidence of abuse of office or conflict of interest regarding accusations linked to the Damang mine and private jet usage.

Story three: Graphic Online reports that Sir Sam Jonah has proposed the establishment of a consensus-based government of national unity to eliminate recurring political tensions during election cycles. Speaking at the Kutu Acheampong Memorial Lectures, he stressed that his proposal would operate strictly within the democratic parameters of Ghana's Fourth Republic.

Story four: Graphic Online also reports on the latest Afrobarometer survey, which reveals a drop in Ghanaian public trust toward political parties. Only 46 percent of citizens now feel close to any political movement, with nearly half of respondents expressing no confidence in either the ruling party or the main opposition.

Now, around the world.

Story five: Africanews reports from South Africa, where Johannesburg has elected Action S A leader Herman Mashaba as its new mayor under a power-sharing arrangement. The deal allows the ruling African National Congress to maintain influence in the city's governance following coalition talks.

Story six: BBC News and France 24 report that an Israeli military strike on a medical clinic in Gaza killed two staff members from Doctors Without Borders. The attack drew strong condemnation from the World Health Organization, while the Israeli military claimed the strike was aimed at Hamas operatives.
