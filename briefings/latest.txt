Good evening. Today is Tuesday, October twenty-seven. First, the news from home.

Story one. The Minority in Parliament has demanded the immediate resignation of Foreign Affairs Minister Samuel Okudzeto Ablakwa over alleged financial irregularities surrounding the evacuation of Ghanaians from South Africa. Three News reports that the Minority has threatened to initiate a vote of censure against him, following calls from anti-graft campaigner Vitus Azeem for a full probe into sixteen point four million Ghana cedis in spending discrepancies.

Story two. Within the National Democratic Congress, National Chairman Johnson Asiedu Nketiah has announced that the party will emerge stronger following its constituency executive elections across the country. Graphic Online reports that while Mr. Asiedu Nketiah praised members for completing the nationwide polls, Starr F M reports that the Yilo Krobo NDC Secretary results remain undeclared due to a ballot recount dispute.

Story three. Turning to the New Patriotic Party, flagbearer Dr. Mahamudu Bawumia has expressed confidence that former General Secretary John Boadu will help lead the party back to power in 2028, according to The Ghana Report. Meanwhile, Graphic Online reports that former Member of Parliament Sarah Adwoa Safo has officially announced her intention to contest the party's upcoming primaries in Dome-Kwabenya.

Now, around the world.

Story four. In Israel, citizens have turned out in record numbers to vote in a pivotal general election that is widely seen as a referendum on Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu. B B C News reports that this is the nation's first election since the October 2023 attacks, with turnout reaching its highest level in decades as voters deliver their verdict on the government's leadership.

Story five. In regional news, Democratic Republic of Congo President Felix Tshisekedi has accused former president Joseph Kabila of acting as a proxy for Rwanda. Africa News reports that President Tshisekedi told lawmakers that Kabila joined forces with the Kigali-backed M twenty-three rebel group, which has been leading an insurgency in the eastern part of the country.
