Good morning. Today is Thursday, October 29. First, the news from home.

Story one. The New Patriotic Party has filed a suit at the Supreme Court seeking an official interpretation of the constitutional provisions on presidential term limits. Reports from Graphic Online and MyJoyOnline state that the N P P wants the court to clarify whether the maximum limit of two terms applies whether those terms are served consecutively or non-consecutively, a decision that could directly affect former President John Dramani Mahama.

Story two. During his trip to Russia, President John Dramani Mahama called for deeper cooperation between Russian transport institutions and Ghanaian engineering and logistics bodies. According to 3 News, Foreign Minister Samuel Okudzeto Ablakwa also revealed that Russia strongly backs Ghana's bid to join the B R I C S group as a partner country, while Canada has offered technical support to establish Ghana's proposed Women's Development Bank.

Story three. The National Nasara Coordinator of the New Patriotic Party, Alhaji Ali Suraj, has announced that the party will prioritize education and infrastructure development in Zongo communities. MyJoyOnline reports that he accused the National Democratic Congress of historically neglecting these areas and relying on material incentives rather than lasting development.

Now, around the world.

Story four. Brazil's defeated presidential candidate, Flávio Bolsonaro, has fled to the United States and declared himself the rightful leader of the country. According to reports from the B B C and France 24, the far-right senator left for Miami after losing Sunday's election runoff to President Luiz Inácio Lula da Silva, while making unproven claims of electoral fraud.

Story five. Israel faces political uncertainty following its latest elections, with partial results showing Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu's bloc falling short of a parliamentary majority. France 24 and the B B C report that with most ballots counted, neither Netanyahu's coalition nor the opposition holds a clear path to govern, raising the possibility of a prolonged impasse or new elections.
